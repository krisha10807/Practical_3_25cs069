How to Capture Screenshots for Microsoft Teams Submission

Follow these quick steps to take high-quality proof screenshots of your output pages:

METHOD 1

Windows Snipping Tool

Press Win + Shift + S on keyboard. Drag your mouse over the page to take a screenshot and paste directly in MS Teams!

METHOD 2

Full-Page Chrome Screenshot

Press F12 → Ctrl + Shift + P → type Capture full size screenshot & hit Enter.

METHOD 3

Mobile / Tablet View Screen

Press F12 → Click Device Toggle Icon (Ctrl+Shift+M) to show responsive preview for mobile view!
